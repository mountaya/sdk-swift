# Contract gaps

Every row is something `mountaya/schemas` leaves open that a Swift, Kotlin or Dart SDK has to
answer in order to exist. None is settled here. Each row says what the contract states today,
what it leaves open, what would close it, and what each SDK does until it lands, so the row can
be lifted into an issue against `schemas` as it stands.

Most rows keep the fixture and runner hygiene the contract leaves open, a fixture whose cases
differ from the wire's shape, a word no case names, and the bindings each platform owns, none of
which changes an answer. The rest are the exception, each a behaviour outside what the fixtures
hold, saying what every SDK does: the instant written for a reader, where the SDKs behave apart;
when a search control shows its list, the corners a right-to-left layout reads, what the safe area
leaves out, the marker's label beside the watermark and off the map, a moment the device's zone
cannot write, what a map with no publishable key answers for its organization, what a control
stack keeps to over a map no view draws, and the watermark rule's public surface, where every SDK
now answers alike; a panel that cannot clear the watermark's frame, where every SDK moves it below
the frame and each still lays out its last resort and cuts it its own way; and the panel a
panel control out of any stack opens, whose space and width each SDK takes its own way. `SPEC.md`
§1 points here in a sentence, and `sdk-kotlin` and `sdk-dart` read the same list and keep no copy:
a gap in the contract is a gap for every SDK.

The contract these were read against is v0.9.0, registry version 20, its model and elements'
data digesting to `f9ded6dd4ca71cb8f89c6974c42adec77509ee9a19e5afd01c31b6ea9dcc33ea`
(`sdk/SPEC.md` §11). A row the contract has since closed is removed rather than kept as history.

## How to read a row

- **States** is what the registry, the fixture or `sdk/SPEC.md` says today.
- **Open** is the question a port author cannot answer from it.
- **Closes it** is the fixture case or the sentence that would.
- **Until then** is what each SDK does, `sdk-swift` where a sentence names none, and is never a
  guess presented as the contract.
